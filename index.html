How to Use

  1. Enter your income and expenses in the Edit panel (click the pencil icon in the sidebar).
  2. Choose a plan that fits your goals — Navigator (balanced), Velocity (aggressive), or Apex (extreme).
  3. Click Save Edits to download your data file to your device.
  4. Click Load Saved to restore your data from that file.
  5. Click Save as Default to remember your setup — the tracker will auto-load it next time you open it, and Reset to Defaults will restore it.
